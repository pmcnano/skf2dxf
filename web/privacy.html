Privacy

← Back to the converter

The data described here is handled by skf2dxf.com. Last updated November 4, 2026.

Drawings you convert

The converter runs inside your browser. The drawings you convert, and the DXF and PDF files it makes, stay on your computer and are never sent to us.

Drawings you send us

If you press "send us this file", we receive the drawing, what the converter reported about it, and the comment and email address you typed, if any. We use them only to fix the converter and, if you left an address, to reply to you. We don't share or sell them. They're stored with Cloudflare and deleted once we've looked into the problem. To have a file you sent deleted sooner, write to us.

To stop the form being abused, your IP address is checked against a short rate limit when you send a file. It isn't stored with the drawing or anywhere else.

Usage counts

To see how well the converter works, the page counts, anonymously: each conversion and whether it succeeded, the AutoSketch version of the file, its rough size, which parts couldn't be converted, and when the ZIP, preview, PDF or send buttons are used, along with your browser's language and your country. No file names, drawing contents, cookies or IP addresses are kept.

We also use Cloudflare Web Analytics, which counts page visits without cookies or tracking you across sites. See Cloudflare's privacy policy.
