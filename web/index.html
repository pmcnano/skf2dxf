    Questions

    What is an SKF file?

    It's a drawing made with AutoSketch, Autodesk's simple 2D drafting program. Autodesk no longer sells or updates AutoSketch, and AutoCAD can't open its .skf files, so older plans and details get stuck in a format nothing else reads.

    How do I open an SKF file in AutoCAD?

    Drop the .skf file above and download the DXF. In AutoCAD, use Open and choose the DXF file type. The same DXF also opens in BricsCAD, DraftSight, LibreCAD, QCAD and most other CAD programs.

    Can I convert SKF to DWG?

    Convert it to DXF here, open the DXF in AutoCAD and save it as DWG. DXF is AutoCAD's own exchange format, so nothing is lost on the way.

    What is kept in the conversion?

    Layers, colors, linetypes, lines, arcs, circles, ellipses, polylines, text, hatches with AutoSketch's patterns, symbols (as blocks) and dimensions (as editable AutoCAD dimensions). Angular dimensions aren't converted yet; when something is left out, the result says what.

    Which AutoSketch versions work?

    It was built and tested with AutoSketch 10 drawings, and many files from earlier versions convert too. If one of yours doesn't, use the "send us this file" link and we'll look at it.

    Are my drawings uploaded?

    No. The converter runs inside your browser, so your files never leave your computer. A drawing is only sent to us if you press "send us this file" yourself.

    Does it cost anything or need an install?

    It's free and needs no account. You can convert many files at once and download them as one ZIP. After the first visit the page also works offline, and your browser may offer to install it as an app.
